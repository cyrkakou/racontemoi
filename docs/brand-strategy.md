# Brand strategy — Racontemoi

**Date:** 2026-11-11 · **Positioning:** premium editorial storytelling, self-funded, French-first

## 1. Name & idea

**Racontemoi** — « tell me a story » in the intimate second person. The brand voice speaks to the
parent as the one who tells; the child is « dedans » — inside the story.

Tagline (FR): **« Le conte que vous avez aimé, raconté avec votre enfant dedans. »**
Descriptor: *Ateliers de livres personnalisés — contes africains pour enfants.*

## 2. Positioning

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| An editorial publisher that personalizes | A print-on-demand gimmick |
| African stories told with cultural care | "African-style" clipart decoration |
| A keepsake digital object first, print later | A commodity photo-book app |
| Quiet, warm, crafted | Loud, gamified, subscription-bait |

Reference class: a small literary press + a bespoke bookbinder — not a SaaS dashboard.

## 3. Audience

- **Primary:** francophone parents of children 3–12, mostly West/Central Africa + diaspora
  (Abidjan, Dakar, Paris, Montréal…), middle income, gift-driven purchases (birthdays, baptêmes,
  beginnings of school year).
- **Job to be done:** « offrir un moment qui compte » — a story that belongs to their child.
- **Trust barriers:** authenticity accusations ("l'Afrique n'est pas un seul pays"), child privacy,
  price vs. perceived value.

## 4. Brand pillars

1. **La-source** — every story declares its cultural grounding honestly (history / inspiration /
   fiction). No generic "Africa".
2. **L'atelier** — personalization is a craft: the parent shapes the tale, approves each page.
3. **La transmission** — books as family objects; stories meant to be re-read and offered onward.

## 5. Voice & tone (FR)

- Warm, precise, unhurried. Short sentences. No exclamation inflation.
- Verbs over adjectives. « Écoutez », « Choisissez », « Approuvez » — the parent is the teller.
- Cultural terms kept with a gloss, never translated away (baobab, griot, savane).
- Errors explain and guide; empty states invite action. No jargon ("génération IA" → « illustration en cours »).

## 6. Visual identity thesis

**"Une bibliothèque au crépuscule"** — an evening library: warm lamplight on paper, hand-painted
plates, serif titles set like a bound volume. Terracotta/ochre are *pigments*, not UI chrome;
green is *botanical*, not "tech green". Restraint is the luxury signal: one memorable element per
screen, everything else quiet.

Anti-references (never): Wonderbly layouts/brand, generic AI-gradient SaaS, stock-photo children,
emoji-as-iconography, 3D glossy renders.

## 7. Business model note (context for design)

Hybrid editorial + AI: catalog first, personalization second, live AI generation is a paid-intent
action only. Demo mode must feel like a real atelier preview, not a trial gimmick — it demonstrates
the whole ritual so the paid step is obvious.
